Glucarb

A fast, private carbohydrate log for Android.

  • Build your own catalog of foods, by weight, volume or portion.
  • Log a meal in a couple of taps.
  • Export your history for later analysis.
  • No account, no ads, no network access. Your data stays on your phone.

Privacy policy

Glucarb is a record-keeping and arithmetic tool. It does not provide medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat or manage any condition.
