Privacy Policy

Glucarb · last updated 8 September 2026

In short: Glucarb collects nothing. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. The app is built without the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your information anywhere.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your catalog of foods, their carbohydrate values, units and portion sizes;
  • any photos you attach to a catalog entry, or take for a one-off estimate;
  • your meal history: what you logged, how much, and when;
  • your app preferences.

No other app can read this data. Uninstalling Glucarb deletes all of it.

What the app sends

Nothing, on its own initiative. Glucarb has no network access.

There are exactly two ways information can leave the app, and you trigger both:

  • The AI estimate feature. When you choose to send a photo of a plate for an estimate, Glucarb hands that single photo to another app that you pick yourself, using the standard Android share mechanism. It also places the accompanying question on the clipboard so you can paste it. What happens to the photo after that is governed by the privacy policy of the app you chose, not by this one. Glucarb receives nothing back except any number you copy across.
  • Export and backup. You can export your meal history as a CSV file, or your whole data set as a backup archive, to a location you select. Those files are yours; Glucarb does not read them afterwards.

Android system backup

If you have Android's automatic backup enabled in your device settings, the system may copy the app's data to your own Google account, in the same way it does for your other apps. This is a function of the operating system and your Google account settings, and the developer of Glucarb has no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Permissions

Glucarb requests no Android permissions. Taking a photo is handled by your existing camera app, and choosing an existing photo is handled by your gallery or file picker, so the app never needs access to your camera or your photo library directly.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. Glucarb contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no tracking of any kind, and it does not read the Android advertising ID.

Children

Glucarb is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. As stated above, it collects no information from any user of any age.
