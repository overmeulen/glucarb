Camera icon — candidates
＋
PhotoCamera, filled — current
Solid mass next to a hairline +. The weights fight; this is what looks wrong.
＋
CameraAlt, outlined — recommended
Same hairline stroke as the +, so the pair reads as one family. Still unmistakably a camera.
＋
CenterFocusStrong
Viewfinder. The prettiest geometry of the four, but it says “focus” more than “photograph my plate”.
＋
AutoAwesome
Says “AI” loudly, says “take a photo” not at all. Wrong first step for the user.
The icon was not the whole problem. The filled camera is a solid blob; the + is a hairline cross. At the same 56 dp they read as different weights, which is what made the pair look unbalanced. Switching to the outlined camera matches the stroke and fixes it without changing the metaphor.
Kept as a camera on purpose. The first thing the user does is take a picture; sparkles would describe what happens afterwards, not what to tap.
The 3-tap happy path — screen 1 → 2: open app (Home, current meal already open) → tap Pasta → tap the 150 g chip → Add to meal. The chip is pre-selected at your last-used quantity, so it is really tap tile → tap Add when nothing changed.
Tiles use your own photos (emoji stand-ins here). No photo → coloured initial tile (see “Yogurt”). Badge shows carbs/100 or the portion unit.
Ordering is frequency × recency bucketed by time of day — the “Suggested · lunchtime” section reshuffles through the day.
Portions (screen 3) default to the portion unit with a Grams toggle; half steps supported; stored normalised to grams.
AI (screen 6): camera FAB → photo → shared to your chosen app with the saved prompt → come back → the clipboard number is parsed and needs one confirm tap.
